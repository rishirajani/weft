# Changelog

Weft follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/) for its terms: a minor version adds
terms or loosens constraints; a major version removes or changes the meaning of a term.
Every version stays available at `https://w3id.org/weft/<version>`.

## 1.0 — 2026-11-05

First public release.

- 2 classes (`weft:Award`, `weft:CareerBreak`), 22 relations and 4 value properties, built on
  schema.org.
- Namespace `https://w3id.org/weft#`; documentation, Turtle, JSON-LD, RDF/XML and N-Triples
  published from this repository.
- A JSON-LD context, SHACL shapes, a made-up example that uses every term, and a checker for
  domains and ranges.
- Licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Weft started as the `gr:` vocabulary of [Graph Resume](https://rishirajani.github.io/Graph-Resume/)
in October 2026. The terms are the same; only the namespace changed, and the descriptions
were made general.
